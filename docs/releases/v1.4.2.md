# v1.4.2 — OBJ08 swap identity hotfix

Corrects swap-state grading in `obj08-05` and proactively hardens
`obj08-06`, `obj08-11`, and `obj08-12`.

The previous grader depended on a `swapon --show` UUID output column and some
future checks depended on friendly device names. Those representations are not
portable across the local RHEL 9.8 toolchain and device-mapper LVM paths.

The new contract enumerates active swap devices by name, resolves every active
entry to its UUID with `blkid`, and grades the training swap by UUID. Each swap
lab also records the UUIDs that were active before the lab and requires all of
them to remain active.

This validates the intended state rather than a presentation detail of one
utility.
