# v1.7.0 — Objective 11 NFS & AutoFS

Adds six runtime-gradeable labs for RHEL network-attached storage.

Coverage:

- NFS export discovery;
- manual NFS mount and unmount;
- persistent NFS through `/etc/fstab`;
- `rw,sync` NFS mount options;
- direct AutoFS maps;
- indirect AutoFS wildcard maps with `*` and `&`;
- `autofs` enablement and on-demand mounting;
- `x-systemd.automount` generated from `/etc/fstab`;
- integrated NFS/AutoFS challenge.

Infrastructure:

- `serverb` becomes the controlled NFS provider through Ansible;
- all training exports live below `/srv/rhcsa11`;
- normal root squashing is preserved;
- server-side NFS firewall access is prepared automatically.

Automated regression count grows from 86 to 92 labs.
Curriculum count including the two OBJ09 manual-console drills grows from 88
to 94 items.
